Vision & Playbook

The vision, the model, and what's yours to own at the campus level.

HSL Vision

High School Leadership exists to provide intentional discipleship that equips students to be PLANTED in the local church, GROW in their relationship with Jesus, and REACH the lost.

The Model

Central owns the why + what
  • Vision
  • Outcomes
  • Student Expectations
  • Director Expectations — green / yellow / red playbook
  • Approved Content Topics
  • Brand / Look
Campuses own the how
  • Time + Location
  • Experience
  • Contextualization
  • Activation
  • Relational Approach

The HSL Journey

01 Plant

Take ownership of the house.

  • Worship One + Serve One
  • Serve through the Dream Team
  • Connected through Small Groups
  • Coaching from leadership
  • Given opportunities to lead
Planted, dependable, connected, taking ownership.
02 Grow

Take ownership of your faith.

  • Know what & why they believe
  • Biblical foundations
  • Consistent spiritual disciplines
  • Character and integrity
  • Hear from God, respond in obedience
Faith that isn't dependent on a service, leader, or environment.
03 Reach

Take ownership in God's mission.

  • Genuine relationships with the lost
  • Confidently share their faith
  • Pray intentionally for lost friends
  • Identify their mission field
  • Invite people into HSL & services
Students who help other people know Jesus.

Outcomes to Track

Engagement: total HSL students (weekly, via check-in through Rock or Monday), and % with 75%+ attendance.

StageMetrics
Plant% Growth Track complete · % serving on Dream Team · % connected to Small Groups
Grow% leading Small Groups · % given leadership opportunities · % in spiritual disciplines (tracking TBD)
Reach% in school outreach · % who shared their faith · # first-time guests brought

Green / Yellow / Red Playbook

🟢 Green — Campus Ownership
  • Fun + relational elements
  • Student Task Force
  • Campus activations
  • Flow
  • Décor
  • Location + format
  • Budget within expectations
  • Serving expectations
🟡 Yellow — Alignment Needed
  • Student Expectations / Honor Code
  • HSL Values
  • Branding + graphics
  • Content outside approved catalog
  • Changes to semester plan / cadence
🔴 Red — Non-Negotiable
  • Meet consistently
  • Student Director ownership
  • Open to students meeting expectations
  • Clear communication channel
  • Move students toward Plant + Grow + Reach

Content

Central creates an approved HSL Content Catalog — not fully built-out messages: topics, biblical direction, and activation ideas, so directors can contextualize while staying aligned with the HSL vision.

Expectation: freedom comes with preparation. Content should be planned intentionally, not improvised week to week.
